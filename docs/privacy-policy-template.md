## What we collect and why
- **Account:** name, email and password (stored only as a one-way hash) — to run your account and
  send security, receipt and (if you choose) report emails.
- **Health and wellness data you enter:** water intake and goals; medication names, instructions,
  schedules and the doses you log; workouts — only to provide the app's features and your reports.
- **Location:** precise GPS **only while a workout you started is recording**, to draw the route and
  measure distance; and an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) for weather.
- **Calendar (optional):** if you connect Google Calendar we read only when you are busy or free —
  never event titles, attendees or details. With your approval we add the specific events you choose.
- **Health platforms (optional):** workouts you allow us to read from Apple Health / Health Connect.
- **Purchases:** subscription status and receipts from Apple / Google via RevenueCat. We never see
  your card details.

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or include advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Medication emails
By default, medication report emails show only an overall summary. Medication names and the PDF are
included only if you turn that on (Profile → Email & reports).

## Your choices and rights
- Change or turn off report emails at any time (Profile → Email & reports, or the unsubscribe link).
- Disconnect Google Calendar or a health app at any time; we revoke our access.
- **Get a copy** of your data: Profile → Privacy & data → Email me my data.
- **Delete your account** and its data: Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account. Also cancel any
  Premium subscription in your App Store / Google Play account.
- For anything else contact [PRIVACY EMAIL]. You may also complain to your data-protection
  authority ([e.g. the Information Regulator, South Africa]).

## Retention
We keep your data while your account exists. Short-lived security records (sign-in codes, sessions)
are deleted automatically. Deleting your account removes your data from our live systems straight
away and from backups within [30] days.

## Security
HTTPS in transit, passwords hashed with Argon2id, calendar tokens encrypted at rest, and access
limited to what each feature needs.
